Upload your monitoring methodology plan (MMP) here. You should also upload the MMP flow diagram and any other supporting documents for your NER application.

To download the MMP:

  1. Select the relevant account from your dashboard.
  2. Select “Start a variation”.
  3. Complete the “Monitoring methodology plan” section, then save a draft of your permit variation application. Do not submit or cancel the application at this stage.
  4. Download the draft permit preview PDF and upload it here.
  5. Once your NER application has been approved, you can return to the saved draft and submit the permit variation application.

Contact your regulator if you have any questions.
