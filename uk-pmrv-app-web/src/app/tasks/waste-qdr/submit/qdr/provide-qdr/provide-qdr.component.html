Taking part in the voluntary MRV-only period will allow operators to provide data in relation to their installation which will be used to support policy decisions on MRV methodologies. This data is necessary to facilitate the development and testing of granular emissions factors to support the pass through of ETS costs from waste incinerators to their customers.
If you choose yes, you will:
  • Download the quarterly report template.
  • Fill it in as best you can.
  • Upload it with any supporting files, such as CEMS raw data or C14 raw data.
