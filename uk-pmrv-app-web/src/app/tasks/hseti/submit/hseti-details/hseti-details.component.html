For the purpose of an application to increase an installation’s emissions targets, you must divide the installation into sub-installations in accordance with Article 10 of the Free Allocation Regulation (this will normally fall under the Heat sub-installation).
To make an application, you must complete and upload the HSE target increase template. <Contact your regulator> to request a copy of the template. Your application must contain:
  • evidence of the capacity increase and the sub-installation to which it relates, including evidence that it has been put into operation and is not temporary
  • evidence of any capacity decrease at the installation since the reference date and the sub-installation to which it relates, including, where relevant, evidence that it is temporary
  • evidence to enable the combined capacity utilisation factor to be calculated, as set out in Schedule 7, paragraph 19F, of the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020
  • where the capacity increase or any capacity decrease relates to a district heating sub-installation or a heat benchmark sub-installation of an installation that consumes or exports measurable heat produced outside the installation, provide evidence of whether or not the capacity increase or capacity decrease is solely associated with measurable heat produced at the installation
