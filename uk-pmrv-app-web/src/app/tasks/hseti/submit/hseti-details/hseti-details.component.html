Your application must contain:

  • evidence of any capacity decrease at the installation since the reference date and the sub-installation to which it relates, including, where relevant, evidence that it is temporary
  • evidence to enable the combined capacity utilisation factor to be calculated, as set out in paragraph 19F of Article 25 of the Greenhouse Gas Emissions Trading Scheme Order 2020
