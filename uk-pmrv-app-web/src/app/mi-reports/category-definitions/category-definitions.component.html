Category Definitions

Choosing a category

Most reports fit under a single category, but some reports cover more than one purpose. You can select multiple categories that are relevant to your report.

Categories

Workflow submission status

Reports that track where an individual submission, such as an AER, ALR, BDR, VIR or AIR, has reached in its process, such as due dates, assignees, or the status of any outstanding items. Use this when the report covers what point a workflow has reached, rather than describing the account as a whole.

Management

Summary or oversight reports used to manage the service or scheme, such as account status listings, counts of applications by type, or activity summarised across many accounts. Use this for broad service-level views rather than the detail of a single submission.

Compliance

Reports that assess whether an operator or account is meeting its obligations under the UK ETS, including emissions report content, verification findings, permit conditions, and non-compliance or penalty details.

Reconciliation

Reports that compare or align METS data with another source, most commonly the UK ETS Registry, such as emissions figures prepared for registry submission or account data cross-checked against registry records.

Users and contacts

Reports about the people and addresses associated with an account, such as authorised users, primary and finance contacts, and registered or operational addresses.
